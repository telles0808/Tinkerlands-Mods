# 🧭 TomTom (v1.0) - Tinkerlands Mod

**ID:** `5004`  
**Author:** Telles0808  
**Compatibility:** Tinkerlands (All versions)

---

## 📖 Overview
**TomTom** is the ultimate navigation and awareness suite for Tinkerlands, seamlessly combining a real-time NPC & Player Sonar Radar with an interactive fullscreen map waypoint system.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📡 1. In-Game HUD Sonar Radar
* **NPC & Player Tracking:** Shows directional arrows with character portraits and live distance indicators along the screen borders.
* **In-Screen Entity Identification:** Displays NPC portraits and names directly above entities when they enter your field of view.
* **HUD Sonar Toggle:** Click the Sonar icon beside the minimap anytime to enable/disable the screen-edge radar.

### 📍 2. Interactive Map Waypoints (Pins)
* **4 Distinct Pin Categories:** Waypoint (📍), Storage (📦), Point of Interest (❓), Boss / Danger (💀).
* **Drag & Drop Canvas Placement:** Easily drag pins from the top-left palette onto any map coordinate.
* **Move & Reorganize:** Click and drag existing pins across the map canvas; pins follow the cursor in real time.
* **Illuminated Trash Bin:** Drag pins into the glowing trash bin icon in the top-left corner to delete them.
* **Live Dynamic TomTom Guidance:** All active map pins project directional arrows, custom category icons, and distance indicators directly onto your HUD during normal gameplay.
* **Tile Coordinate Displays:** Crisp coordinate labels `(X, Y)` are displayed below each placed marker.

### 🌐 3. Player Coordinate Display
* **RealClock-Aligned GPS:** Shows your real-time tile coordinates `(X, Y)` in the bottom-right corner of your screen in native pixel style.

### 💾 4. Persistence
* Automatically saves and loads all placed markers to/from `tomtom_pins.cfg`.

---

## 📥 Installation
1. Copy `TomTom.mod` into your Tinkerlands `mods/` directory:
   ```
   C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Tinkerlands\mods\
   ```
2. Clear cache: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tinkerlands\temp`
3. Launch Tinkerlands and press **M** to start placing markers!
